Our Story

Based in Colombo, Sri Lanka, ASHADI CEYLON GEMS represents the pinnacle of fine Jewellery craftsmanship, where each piece tells a story of timeless elegance and meticulous artistry. Founded on the principles of exceptional quality and innovative design, we create heirloom pieces that transcend generations.

Our portfolio represents over 38+ years of experience working with Ceylon sapphires and fine gemstones, collaborating with private clients, collectors, and international partners. Our master artisans blend traditional techniques with contemporary aesthetics, sourcing only the finest ethically-mined gemstones and precious metals rooted in the island's world-renowned gemstone heritage.
